Ferrite Frontier

Privacy policy. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

The short version

Ferrite Frontier does not collect, send or share any personal data. Your progress stays on your own device.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (finished contracts, best scores, saved layouts, credits, perks and your settings such as sound and motion) in the local storage of your browser or of the Android app. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it with Reset progress in Settings, by clearing the site data of your browser, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings.

What is not collected

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or usage statistics.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, tracking cookies or profiling.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

The game has no server of its own. Its fonts are built into the page, so playing does not contact other websites. The Android app declares the internet permission only because that is the default of its app framework; the game does not use it. The game may give a short vibration as feedback (you can switch this off in Settings).

Save codes

Settings can show a save code that you can copy to move your progress to another device. The code is created on your device and is only shared when you choose to copy and paste it yourself. The developer never sees it.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes and contact

If the game ever starts to collect data (for example if ads or purchases are added), this page will be updated first. Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
